Statistics Homework

Statistics course — assignments, code and write-ups

Assignments

  • HMWK 1

    Public-Key Cryptography: Private Key → Public Key

    Elliptic-curve scalar multiplication on a toy curve y² = x³ + 7 (mod 17), with step-by-step visualization and a statistical exploration of the results.
  • HMWK 2

    From Wallet Seed to Ethereum Address

    The Ethereum wallet pipeline computed live: BIP-39 mnemonic and seed, BIP-32/BIP-44 key derivation, secp256k1, Keccak-256 and the EIP-55 checksum, with their history and a statistical analysis of every stage.
  • HMWK 2.1

    Toy ECC Encryption vs. the Caesar Cipher

    Encrypting a message with a toy ECIES-like scheme on secp256k1 and with the Caesar cipher, statistical cryptanalysis of Caesar (frequencies, χ², n-grams) and key-search attacks on the restricted ECC key space.
  • HMWK 3

    n Systems, m Attackers: Penetration Scores, LLN and CLT

    The penetration process as a random walk with Bernoulli and Rademacher increments: simulated trajectories, Sn, Sn/n and Sn/√n, the Law of Large Numbers, the Central Limit Theorem and the effect of the number of attackers.
  • HMWK 4

    Welford's Recurrences and the Sampling Mean and Variance

    Derivation and code of Welford's online formulas for the mean, the sum of squares and the variance; m hackers scoring n systems from 0 to 10, and the sampling mean and variance as unbiased, consistent estimators of μ and σ².
